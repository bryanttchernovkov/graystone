# Graystone

A universe for [Unmapped](https://github.com/bryanttchernovkov/unmapped). Content format **2**.

Graystone, a Midwest county seat, where the Mayor suspended the misdemeanor docket and an
all-female Sheriff's Department corrects men on the spot — or for a few days in the Undercroft,
the lockup in the crypt of the old church they work from. You are new in town.

**Adult content (fiction, consenting adults only).** See `bible.md` for the premise, tone,
palette and hard limits, and `cast.md` for camera rules, the uniform and every character's look.

## Maturity

Graystone is marked `maturity: mature`: the Unmapped Marketplace labels it and asks for a
confirmation before installing. Its cover and tagline are safe for work; the content inside is not.
